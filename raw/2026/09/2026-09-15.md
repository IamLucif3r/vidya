# Getting back to zone after 3 days holiday!

## Spotlight

1. **FUZZING** :
  1. Finding vulnerabilities in applications and services is the first step toward developing your own zero-day exploit. Vulnerability Scanners good for KNOWN vulnerabilities, but to find zero-days --> Fuzzer.
  2. ***Tool***: American Fuzzy Lop plus plus ([AFLplusplus](https://github.com/AFLplusplus/AFLplusplus))
2. **DDRop**: A new hardware attack, that breaks memory protection in intel and AMD confidential computing by silently dropping writes to a server's memory, so the processor keeps reading old encrypted data as if it were current.
